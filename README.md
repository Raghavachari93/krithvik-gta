# Neon Bay

An original open-world crime-action game that runs in the browser. Built with TypeScript, Three.js (WebGL) and React, using custom arcade physics and fully procedural audio and art (no external assets).

## Run it

```
npm install
npm run dev        # then open http://localhost:5173
```

Or double-click `start-game.bat`. To make a static build, run `npm run build` (output goes to `dist/`).

## What's in it

- **City**: Downtown towers, Palmetto Heights homes, Sapphire Hills villas, the Neon Strip clubs, Market Row shops, Rustwater industry, Ocean Drive and the beach, the port, the airport, and Coral Key island across a cable-stayed bridge.
- **Atmosphere**: day/night cycle; dynamic weather (rain, storms with lightning, fog, wet roads); lit windows and neon at night; bloom; shadows.
- **On foot**: walking, running, sprinting, jumping, climbing ledges, swimming, fall damage, dodge roll, punching, blocking, a bat, pistol, SMG and shotgun.
- **Vehicles**: 12 vehicles including sports cars, a supercar, a muscle car, an SUV, a van, a box truck, a motorbike, a taxi, police cars, a SWAT van and two boats. Each has its own handling, plus drifting, damage, fire and explosions, fuel, and breakable street props.
- **City life**: pedestrians who walk, cross at signals, chat, flee and phone the police. AI traffic follows lanes and traffic lights.
- **Police**: 6-star wanted system, witnesses, pursuit, search zones, roadblocks, SWAT units and helicopters.
- **Story**: 6 missions (vehicle delivery, package collection, street race, chase & escape, investigation, boat finale).
- **Activities**: 2 street races, 2 time trials, taxi shifts, 8 stunt jumps, 30 Neon Tokens, a drift challenge, a rooftop parkour course, 3 treasure hunts and random encounters.
- **Economy**: dealerships, a garage, buyable properties with daily income, a weapon shop, gas stations, a repair & respray shop, diners and clubs. Also achievements, stats, and save/load.

## Controls

WASD move/drive · Mouse look · Shift sprint · Space jump/climb/handbrake · E interact · F enter/exit vehicle ·
C camera · LMB attack/fire · RMB aim/block · Q dodge · 1-5 weapons · R reload/radio · H medkit/horn ·
L siren · T taxi job · M map · I inventory · Tab pause
